# MahaKrishi Link - Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Title & Theme 
* **Project Name:** MahaKrishi Link
* **Problem Statement:** SIH26132
* **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
* **Vision:** End-to-end digital integration from farm gate to export terminal.

## Slide 2: Problem Realities 
* **Middlemen Exploitation:** Non-transparent pricing structures isolating farmers from actual market value.
* **Grading Disputes:** Subjective, manual quality assessments leading to trust deficits.
* **High Freight Costs:** Unoptimized LTL (Less-than-Truckload) shipping causing empty return trips and high logistics overhead.

## Slide 3: Solution Architecture 
* **The MahaKrishi Ecosystem (6 Integrated Modules):**
  1. KrishiVision AI (Quality)
  2. Kisan-Vyapar Setu (Marketplace)
  3. MahaBhav (Analytics)
  4. BhashaKisan (Accessibility)
  5. MahaVahan (Logistics)
  6. Government Command (Governance)
* **Architecture Flow:** Unified microservices architecture enabling seamless data flow across all modules.

## Slide 4: KrishiVision AI Quality Assessment
* **Core Tech:** Edge-based Computer Vision.
* **Function:** Automated, instantaneous crop grading via smartphone cameras.
* **Impact:** Standardized, tamper-proof quality reports that build buyer trust and eliminate manual disputes.

## Slide 5: Kisan-Vyapar Setu B2B Reverse Bidding
* **Core Tech:** Real-time WebSocket auction engine.
* **Function:** Transparent auction marketplace connecting FPOs directly with bulk buyers.
* **Impact:** Maximizes price realization for farmers by removing intermediary commissions.

## Slide 6: MahaBhav Price Forecasting
* **Core Tech:** Time-series ML models trained on historical APMC data.
* **Function:** Predictive price analytics and market trend visualization.
* **Impact:** Empowers farmers with actionable intelligence on *when* and *where* to sell for optimal returns.

## Slide 7: BhashaKisan Marathi Voice Assistant
* **Core Tech:** NLP / Vernacular Speech-to-Text.
* **Function:** Fully voice-driven app navigation and query resolution in native Marathi.
* **Impact:** Bridges the digital divide, ensuring a zero learning curve for rural users.

## Slide 8: MahaVahan Shared Logistics Pooling
* **Core Tech:** GIS routing & capacity aggregation algorithms.
* **Function:** Route optimization (e.g., Lasalgaon -> JNPT) matching available truck capacity with FPO freight needs.
* **Impact:** Drastically reduces transport costs and increases fleet utilization rates.

## Slide 9: Government Command Dashboard & Impact Metrics
* **Core Tech:** Real-time data visualization & Big Data processing.
* **Function:** Macro-level visibility into crop movement, trade volumes, and fair-trade compliance.
* **Impact:** Enables data-driven policymaking and proactive supply chain interventions.

## Slide 10: 3-Step Live Demo Flow
1. **Assess:** Farmer runs KrishiVision scan to instantly list verified produce.
2. **Match:** Buyer accepts the listing via the Setu B2B engine.
3. **Move:** MahaVahan pools the order into an active corridor (Route 101) with live map tracking.
